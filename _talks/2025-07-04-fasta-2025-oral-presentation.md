Delivered the conference presentation as first author, reporting research on predictive observer-compensated high-order fully actuated tracking control for a linear switched reluctance machine.

Also participated in organizing the thematic exchange “Applications of Fully Actuated System Theory in Motor Control and Optimization,” covering nonlinear control, predictive control, multi-motor coordination, parameter identification, and intelligent control.
