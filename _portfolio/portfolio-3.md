This project integrates perception, planning, and control for autonomous takeoff, target traversal, obstacle avoidance, and vision-guided landing.

The platform combines PX4, ROS, MAVROS, RflySim, EGO-Planner, depth cameras, and LiDAR. The related research also includes participation in the development of an online-optimized predictive attitude-control framework for quadrotor UAVs.
