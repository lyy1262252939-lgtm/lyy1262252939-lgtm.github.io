## Selected Research

<ul class="highlight-list">
  <li><strong>PMSM robotic joint servo systems:</strong> developed an integrated state-estimation and predictive-control framework under random noise, communication delays, and load disturbances; conducted motor–load modeling, parameter identification, and FOC-based implementation on the TI C2000 F28379D platform.</li>
  <li><strong>Linear switched reluctance machines:</strong> developed predictive observer-compensated high-order fully actuated control methods.</li>
  <li><strong>Quadrotor UAVs:</strong> participated in an online-optimized predictive attitude-control framework and an autonomous platform integrating perception, planning, and control with PX4, ROS, MAVROS, RflySim, EGO-Planner, depth cameras, and LiDAR.</li>
</ul>
