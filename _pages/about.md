I am currently an M.Eng. student in Control Science and Engineering at Shenzhen University, supervised by Prof. Li Qiu. I received my B.Eng. degree in Electrical Engineering and Intelligent Control from Dongguan University of Technology.

My research focuses on high-precision servo control and state estimation for robotic systems, with particular interests in predictive control, motor-drive systems, learning-enhanced control, and active vibration suppression.

I have conducted research on PMSM-based robotic joint servo systems, linear switched reluctance machines, and quadrotor UAV control. My work involves system modeling, parameter identification, state estimation, predictive control, embedded implementation, and experimental validation.

## Research Interests

- High-Precision Robotic Servo Control
- Predictive Control and Model Predictive Control
- State Estimation and Observer Design
- PMSM Drives and Motor Control
- Learning-Enhanced Control
- Active Vibration Suppression
- Autonomous UAV Control

## Research Highlights

- Developed predictive control and state-estimation methods for PMSM-based robotic joint servo systems.
- Conducted embedded implementation and experimental validation on the TI C2000 F28379D platform.
- Developed high-order fully actuated predictive control methods for linear switched reluctance machines.
- Participated in the development of autonomous UAV systems integrating perception, planning, and control.
- Received the National First Prize in the simulation track and the National Third Prize in the real-robot track of the 2025 CICC Embodied Intelligence Algorithm Challenge.
