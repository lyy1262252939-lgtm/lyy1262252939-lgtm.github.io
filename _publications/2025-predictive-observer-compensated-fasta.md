## Abstract

The linear switched reluctance machine (LSRM) has gained prominence in precision motion control owing to its structural simplicity, reliability, and cost-efficiency. To enhance tracking performance, this paper introduces a predictive observer-compensated strategy for high-order fully actuated (HOFA) control of the LSRM. A HOFA system is formulated by discretizing the dynamic model of the LSRM. Moreover, a predictive HOFA observer is designed to estimate the unmeasurable position state of the LSRM. Through solving the generalized Sylvester equation, parameterized expressions for closed-loop eigenvectors and position feedback matrices are derived. This approach enables the transformation of the closed-loop LSRM system into a constant high-order linear system with prescribed eigenstructure. The capability and advantage of the predictive HOFA observer method are illustrated through simulations involving three LSRMs with different initial positions.
