## Abstract

Linear switched reluctance machine (LSRM) is gaining attention in precision motion control applications due to its simple structure and high reliability. This study aims to achieve high-accuracy trajectory tracking for LSRM through refined dynamic modeling and control design. The LSRM is modeled from a low-order physical system to a high-order strict-feedback system (HOSFS), finally transforming into a high-order fully actuated (HOFA) model. A recursive controller is designed using parametric configuration to shape the closed-loop dynamics. Simulation results validate the proposed method’s effectiveness in delivering high-performance tracking control for LSRM, specifically in accurately tracking harmonic signals.
