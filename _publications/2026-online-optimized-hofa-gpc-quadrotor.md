## Abstract

This paper addresses the high-precision attitude tracking task of a quadrotor UAV in the small-angle region by developing an online-optimized high-order fully actuated generalized predictive control (HOFA-GPC) framework. To improve tracking accuracy and stability, the quadrotor rotational dynamics are reformulated from a strict-feedback form into an equivalent discrete-time HOFA representation. On this basis, a stabilizing feedback control law is constructed via parameter configuration, and a tracking-error system is established through a δ-error reformulation. A multi-step prediction model is then derived using the Diophantine equation approach, and a corresponding cost function is formulated to enhance tracking precision. The resulting constrained predictive optimization problem is equivalently reformulated as an LMI-constrained problem via the Schur complement. This yields a receding-horizon HOFA-GPC framework with online-updated control actions at each sampling instant. Closed-loop stability is established by Lyapunov stability analysis. Numerical simulations and software-in-the-loop (SITL) experiments, including comparisons with MPC, HOFA, and PID baselines under actuator-channel noise, demonstrate that the proposed framework achieves high-precision attitude tracking with smooth control inputs and favorable implementation feasibility.
