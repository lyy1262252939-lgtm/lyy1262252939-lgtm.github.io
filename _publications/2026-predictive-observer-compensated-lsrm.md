## Abstract

The linear switched reluctance machine (LSRM) has attracted significant interest in precision motion control owing to its simple structure, high reliability, and cost-effectiveness. This paper proposes a predictive observer-compensated high-order fully actuated (HOFA) control strategy to enhance its tracking performance. A discrete-time HOFA model is established from the LSRM dynamics. Within a two-degree-of-freedom eigenstructure assignment framework, the controller and observer are co-designed with independent tuning flexibility, enabling separate shaping of tracking and estimation dynamics. The corresponding controller and observer gains are analytically parameterised by solving a generalised Sylvester equation, thereby avoiding iterative optimisation and yielding a high-order closed-loop system with a prescribed eigenstructure. The proposed approach enables an explicit computation of the control gains through an analytical formulation, which significantly simplifies controller design, analysis, and practical implementation. Simulation results on an LSRM system demonstrate that the proposed controller achieves superior performance compared with several existing methods.
