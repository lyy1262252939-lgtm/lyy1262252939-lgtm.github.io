## Abstract

Random noise, network-induced delay, and load-torque variations jointly degrade the position-servo performance of permanent magnet synchronous motor (PMSM) drives in networked control system (NCS). This article proposes a high-order fully actuated (HOFA) estimation and predictive control strategy for networked PMSM drives. A local HOFA stabilizing controller is first designed to render the nominal motor-side position loop stable. At the networked controller side, the HOFA Kalman filter (HKF) fuses model-based position prediction with network-transmitted position feedback to attenuate random feedback noise and improve controller-side feedback reliability. Based on the fused position estimate, the HOFA predictive control (HOFAPC) generates a delay-compensated predictive input to enhance tracking accuracy and robustness against network-induced delay and load-torque variations. Experimental results on a 64 W dual-motor test bench demonstrate improved position-servo performance under random feedback noise, network-induced delay, and load-torque variations.
